About EveSynapse

About

EveSynapse is a fleet management platform for EVE Online. Every character you own, in one place: skills, wallets, assets, industry, planetary colonies, mail and market activity, watched together and turned into briefings, build plans and trade ideas. It is greater than the sum of its parts because the parts know about each other.

The first version was built in 2013. This one is a ground-up rebuild in Go: server-rendered pages, computations done ahead of time instead of at page load, designed to stay fast with a thousand pilots and all their alts tracked on one small server. Corporations and alliances can also run their own instance.

EveSynapse is developed in Seattle, WA by natemsz and operated by Synap6.io LLC, dba Synap6. The code is open source under the BSD-3-Clause-Clear license, at github.com/natemsz/evesynapse.

What we collect

Sign-in happens through EVE Online's official SSO. We never see or store your EVE password.

When you link a character, EVE issues this instance an access token covering the permissions (scopes) you approved on the sign-in page. Tokens are stored encrypted at rest. You choose the permissions; linking another character never widens an existing character's permissions.

For each linked character we store what ESI returns under those permissions: identity, skills and skill queues, wallet journals and balances, assets, mail, contracts, industry jobs, planetary colonies and similar game data. It is refreshed on a schedule so that pages load from stored data rather than live requests.

We also collect public game data, such as market prices and item and universe data. It is not tied to any account.

Cookies and your browser

One session cookie keeps you signed in. Your theme choice and home page layout are stored in your own browser's local storage. There are no advertising cookies, and the site runs no third-party analytics or trackers. Browser push notifications are opt-in, and you can turn them off on the Notifications page or in your browser at any time.

How data is used

Character data is used to render your pages and compute your briefings and alerts. The Trade Ideas feature sends aggregated public market statistics to an AI model provider for analysis; no character or account data is ever included in those requests.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share character data with third parties. Instances of EveSynapse may exchange public market data with peer instances to keep prices fresh; character data never leaves the instance it was linked to. We disclose data only when required by law.

Your control

You can unlink a character on the Characters page at any time. Unlinking deletes that character's stored data and tokens immediately. You can also revoke this instance's access from your EVE account's third-party applications page, which invalidates the stored tokens. To delete your whole account, email evesynapse@pm.me and we will remove it.
