{{/* The abyssal pricing explainer: its argument is the visible text; hovering it, focusing it, or tapping it on a touch screen shows how the figure is made. One wording, wherever an abyssal estimate is named. */}} {{define "abyssal-howto"}}{{.}} How EveSynapse prices Abyssal modules 1. Score the rolls. Each roll is placed in the range its mutaplasmid could have produced and weighted by how much that stat matters for the module. Fitting costs only count against it, and a bricked main stat caps the score. 2. Place it on the ladder. The same measure is taken of the ordinary modules of its kind and size (Tech II, faction, deadspace, officer), whose stats are fixed and whose prices come from deep markets. The module is priced between the two it falls between. 3. Cap it at making another. Never more than the expected cost of rolling one at least this good: attempts × (base module + mutaplasmid). 4. Let clean listings nudge, never decide. Contract asks move a family's figures by at most −20% / +25%, and only once enough clean listings agree. Bundles, exchanges, stale listings and one-seller floods don't count. value = ladder(index) × market factor [0.8–1.25], ≤ recreation cost {{end}}
