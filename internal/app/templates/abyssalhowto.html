{{/* The abyssal pricing explainer: its argument is the visible text; hovering it, focusing it, or tapping it on a touch screen shows how the figure is made. One wording, wherever an abyssal estimate is named. */}} {{define "abyssal-howto"}}{{.}} How EveSynapse prices Abyssal modules 1. Score the rolls. Each roll is placed in the range its mutaplasmid could have produced and weighted by how much that stat matters for the module. Fitting costs only count against it, and a bricked main stat caps the score. 2. Place it on the ladder. The same measure is taken of the ordinary modules of its kind and size (Tech II, faction, deadspace, officer), whose stats are fixed and whose prices come from deep markets. The module is priced between the two it falls between. 3. Learn what buyers pay for, where it's proven. For kinds with enough clean listings and confirmed sales, a small model learns what the ladder misses: a correction that can never price a better roll lower and never moves the figure below 0.6× or above 1.6×. It's used only if it beat the plain ladder on newer listings it never saw. Elsewhere, listings only nudge a kind's figures by −20% / +25% at most. Bundles, exchanges, stale listings and one-seller floods never count. 4. Cap it at making another. Never more than the expected cost of rolling one at least this good on the cheapest base and mutaplasmid, after reselling the misses. value = ladder(index) × correction [0.6–1.6] or market factor [0.8–1.25], ≤ recreation cost {{end}}
