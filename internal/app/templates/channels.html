Intel, shared and on the map

An intel channel is a live stream of sightings — “five hostiles in 1DQ1-A”, “Amamake clear” — shared with the pilots, corporations, alliances or groups you choose. Report from the map, paste lines straight from an in-game intel chat, or post a scan. Every report lands on the map for everyone in the channel, fading over half an hour, and routes steer around it.

EveSynapse Axon

Axon is an open source tool that runs beside the game and posts your in-game intel channels here as you play: it reads the chat logs EVE writes, so nothing is pasted by hand. Name a site channel the same as the in-game one (or map them in its config), and every line naming a system becomes a report.
