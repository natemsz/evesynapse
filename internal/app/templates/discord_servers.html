The EveSynapse bot can be added to the Discord server of a corporation or an alliance. There it gives roles to members who have connected their Discord account, and posts new ops in a channel. A corporation's servers are set up by its directors, an alliance's by the directors of its executor corporation.

Create groups A rule can give a role to everyone in a group you keep by hand.

Add as many rules as you have roles to give; a member gets every role they qualify for and loses each one when they stop qualifying. The bot can only give roles that sit below its own in the server's role list. Groups are kept on the Groups page. Which channels a role can open is set under Channels, below.
