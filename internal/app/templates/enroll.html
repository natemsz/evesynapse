Admin accounts sign in with a key

A security key is a small device you plug in or tap, and a passkey lives on your phone or computer; either one proves it is you when you sign in.

This is separate from your EVE account's own two-factor sign-in. Use a different key than the one guarding your EVE account, so the two share no single point of failure.

You get ten recovery codes. They are the only way back in short of the operator, so keep them somewhere safe.

Keys are per hostname: a key enrolled here ({{.Host}}) does not work on any other EveSynapse address.

Recovery codes

Write these down now. This is the only time they are shown. Each code signs you in once, then burns.
