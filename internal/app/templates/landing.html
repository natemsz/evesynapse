Trust

Your keys, your call.

Prices on this site come from EveSynapse's own swept market data, and every price shows where it came from. The same care runs through the whole app: you stay in charge of your characters and your data.

You pick the scopes

Linking a character starts with the features you want: the sign-in asks for the scopes behind those features, and no more. Re-linking the same character never takes a permission away.

Encrypted at rest

Refresh tokens are sealed with AES-256-GCM, the current standard for encryption at rest. The encryption keys are stored off-server, on secure hardware. Reading your data never means trusting us with the keys to it.

Public data, no login

Tools using public data require no login at all to use: the fitting simulator, planners, market and intel tools, and the universe map, are open to everyone. Sign in only when you want your own characters in the picture.

Or run it yourself

Open source, one setup script, your own ESI application and your own data. The public site runs the same build you can download.

PKCE on every sign-in

The EVE login flow is PKCE-protected: the sign-in code can only be exchanged by the browser that asked for it, so an intercepted code is worthless to anyone else.

Revoke on unlink

When you unlink a character, you can fully revoke its token with EVE itself, right in the unlink flow. Deleting our copy is the floor; killing the token at the source is your call.

Up close

The six sections, up close.

The inventory above, one section at a time. Each tab shows the section's headline tool in the picture and the rest of the section beside it, with the connections that make the pieces worth more together.

Fleet Overview

Every character you link, on one screen: fleet worth, wallets, training and what needs doing, priced and sorted before the page opens.

  • Wallet and Journal Every character's wallet and journal in one ledger, so a payment on any alt shows up in the same place the briefing reads.
  • Assets Search every hangar, ship and container across the account at once; the values come from the same stored prices the overview totals.
  • Mail, Calendar and Contacts Mail and events from all your characters in one inbox and one calendar; unread mail that matters lands in the morning briefing.
  • Skill Plans Lay out the train for a goal and watch the queue against it, using the same synced skills the fitting simulator runs on.

Fitting Simulator

A full dogma simulation in the browser: drag modules onto the ring and every number, from DPS to capacitor, recalculates on the spot.

  • Saved Fits and Public Fits Fits you save sit next to your pilots and price themselves from stored market data; publish one and anyone can fly the same numbers.
  • Save to EVE Push a finished fit straight into the game from the simulator, so the fit you tested is the fit you undock.
  • Doctrines A director can promote an ordinary fit into a corp doctrine here, and from then on it lives on the doctrines page for every member.
  • Ship Replacement Replacement claims check the lost ship against the doctrine fit, so payouts follow the fit the corp actually flies.

Build Planner

Read a blueprint and the whole tree is costed before you commit: build or buy on every component, job fees included, your hangars checked first.

  • PI Planner Follow a chain from extractor to finished goods, priced from the same stored market data the Build Planner costs with.
  • Restock Planner Watches your market stock and calls out the lines running low, priced off the same stored sweeps.
  • Blueprints and Job Tracking Your blueprints and running jobs in one place; a finished job lands in the briefing alongside everything else that changed.

Appraisal

Paste a cargo scan, a contract or a whole hangar and every line comes back priced, with the source and the age of the price on the line.

  • Spread Scanner Reads every hub's order books and ranks the margins; the same swept orders feed the prices the rest of the site shows.
  • Tradefinder Routes between regions and prices both ends of the haul before you undock.
  • Trade Ideas Reads every hub sweep and writes up what to trade or build, with the item, the margin and the reasoning on the page.
  • Market Orders Your orders across every hub, watched as they age; the moment one is outbid it lands in your briefing.
  • Top Stations The stations holding the most open ISK and the most orders, ranked from the stored sweeps.
  • Contract Search Search public contracts across New Eden, and price anything you find with the same stored data the appraisal tool reads.

Doctrines

The corp's official fit sets: directors publish them once, and every fit opens in the fitting tool for the member reading it.

  • Corporation Data Members, wallets, assets and structures in one place, behind the same permissions the rest of the corp stack checks.
  • Groups and Roles The groups your corp runs on; the same groups carry into Discord roles and into who can read and post in an intel channel.
  • Discord Roles follow the corp's groups, and ops posts go out from the same calendar the members read.
  • Ops Calendar One calendar every member can read; an op can name a doctrine, and the fleet forms on the fit the doctrine page holds.

Universe Map and Routes

Fresh reports from your channels draw on the map, and the route planner prices them into the route it draws, steering around reported systems.

Axon Axon runs on a member's own PC and reads the chat logs EVE already saves, posting each new line into the channel on its own. Pairing is a one-time code from the channels page, and the downloads are linked from that page too.

  • Intel Channels A channel belongs to a pilot, a corporation, an alliance or a group, and the owner decides who reads and who can post; channels bind to a corp or alliance under the same permission structure as the rest of the corp stack.
  • Scan Reading Paste a directional scan or a local listing and read the breakdown by ship class, with a link you can share; a reading can be posted into a channel as a report on its system.
  • Wars, Incursions and Faction Warfare Tracked as they happen and kept in stored data, so the pages are ready before you undock; the same fronts feed the map's layers.
  • Killboard Every public kill gets its own page with the fit by slot and the value split; kills feed the threat layer that colours the map.
  • System and Character Pages Every system carries its kill heat and recent history, and a pilot's page shows the reports your channels carry about them.
  • Item Database All of New Eden's items, searchable, priced from the same stored sweeps the market tools read.

Know what it's worth, and what to do next.

Appraisal: paste anything, get a price

A cargo scan, a contract, a whole hangar, a fit off a killmail. One box works out what it is and prices it in the region you pick, with the source on every line. The tool runs with no account, and every result gets a link you can share.

Trade ideas, with the reasoning shown

The market engine watches every hub sweep and an AI analyst reads the result: what to trade, what to build, and why, grounded in your region's real order books.

Scanner and Tradefinder

Industry

Plans that price themselves.

Foresight for industry

Build and PI planners that price from stored market data, check your hangars for what you already own, and only ever ask you to buy what's missing.
