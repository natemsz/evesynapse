Every pilot. One glance.

The home page reads every character you link and puts what matters on one screen: the fleet's worth, a briefing that names what needs doing, and every pilot's training and wallet under it.

Desktop app grade accuracy and features.

This is the fitting simulator exactly as pilots use it. A real fit, run through its pilot's actual skills and implants, every module priced from stored market data. Hover a module and the math is right there, because it is calculated, not drawn.

Data in. Decisions out.

Most tools stop at showing you numbers. EveSynapse reads them for you, and tells you what they're worth doing about.

Trade ideas, not price lists

The market engine watches every hub sweep and an AI analyst reads the result: what to trade, what to build, and why, grounded in your region's real order books.

A briefing, not a dashboard dump

Every character digested into one prioritized briefing: empty skill queues, halted extractors, expiring orders, unread mail that matters. Critical first, noise never.

Foresight for industry

Build and PI planners that price from stored market data, check your hangars for what you already own, and only ever ask you to buy what's missing.

Plans that price themselves.

The Build Planner reads a blueprint and costs the whole tree before you commit a single ISK: what to build, what to buy, where the bottleneck sits, and what the finished thing earns against the market.

An analyst reads every sweep.

The spread scanner reads every hub's order books. Then an AI analyst writes up the best of it: the item, the spread, the daily volume and the estimated profit, in plain words.

Prices with a memory.

Every item page carries its recorded history with it: best buy and sell across all five hub regions, and ninety days of trades charted from stored data, not fetched while you wait.

Three alts, or a hundred multiboxed characters.

It adapts to your playstyle. A 100-character multiboxing setup and your 3 alts get the same treatment: every character syncing in the background, the briefing weighted toward what you actually do, fresh data without a stutter, and without waiting on pages to load.

Your keys, your call.

You pick the scopes

Choose the features you want; the EVE login asks for exactly those scopes, and no more.

Encrypted at rest

Refresh tokens are AES-encrypted in the database. Reading your data never means trusting us with the keys to it.

No login, no problem

The fitting simulator, planners, market and intel tools all work anonymously. Sign up only when you want your own data in them.

Or run it yourself

Open source, one setup script, your own ESI application and your own data. Self-hosting is a supported path, not a hack.
