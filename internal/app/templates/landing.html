Every tool in the box.

Twenty-three modules plus the core pages, all reading the same stored data. The public tools work with no account at all.

Your pilots

  • Fleet Overview: every character on one page, with a daily briefing of what needs attention
  • Wallet: balances and journal for every character in one place
  • Assets: search every character's hangars at once
  • Mail, Calendar and Contacts: read and send mail, answer invites, keep contacts
  • Skill Plans: build a plan, and your queue and trained skills count toward it

Fitting

  • Fitting Simulator: the most advanced desktop-grade fitting simulator, in your browser, with full dogma simulation
  • Saved Fits and community Public Fits, the public ones priced from stored data
  • Doctrines: your corp's fit sets, authored in the simulator
  • Ship Replacement: losses verified from your own killmails and valued automatically, participation tracked
  • Save to EVE: sends a fit straight into the game

Industry

  • Build Planner: optimizes build against buy at every step, finds the bottlenecks, and tracks your inventory
  • PI Planner: simulates the whole chain, extractors to finished goods, taxes included
  • Restock Planner: set a target level per item; what you have listed counts against it
  • Blueprints: your library and every running job in one list

Market

  • Appraisal: paste a list, a fit or a cargo scan; every line priced with its source
  • Spread Scanner: ranks the spread at every market hub
  • Tradefinder: finds a route and prices both ends of it
  • Trade Ideas: an AI analyst reads the market data and posts its ideas
  • Market Orders: track where your profit is coming from, and see what is really making you money
  • Top Stations: stations ranked by open orders and the ISK in them
  • Contract Search: the most advanced contract search available, using our proprietary pricing model to find deals on blueprints, abyssals and more

Corporation

  • Corporation Data: members, wallets, assets and structures in one place
  • Groups: hand-kept pilot lists that feed Discord roles
  • Discord: roles follow corp roles, ops are announced, alerts arrive as DMs
  • Ops Calendar: every op on one calendar, shared with the whole corp

Intel and reference

  • Wars, Incursions and Faction Warfare: declared wars, Sansha incursions and the faction front, tracked live
  • Universe Map: threat, kill and sovereignty layers on the game's own layout
  • Killboard: every public kill, streamed in as it lands
  • Route Planner: routes drawn against live threat data, sent to your autopilot
  • D-Scan Parser: paste a scan, get the breakdown and a link to share
  • Intel Channels: intelligently tracks gate camps, known hostile pilots and local; corporate and alliance intel in a shared dashboard you control, fully integrated with Discord and the map
  • Axon: reads in-game intel chat and posts it to your channels and the map
  • Hostile Pilots Tracker: pilots your channels watch, with where each was last seen
  • Item Database: every item, with prices at the five hubs and its price history
What sets us apart

One connected system.

Every tool reads the same characters and the same stored data, and every character syncs in the background. A solo pilot gets the public tools; a medium-sized group gets doctrines, ship replacement and intel channels in the same place, with nothing extra to host.

Fitting in the browser

A full fitting simulator in a browser tab: full dogma simulation, T3 subsystem morphing from live game data, and Save to EVE built in. Nothing to install.

Prices we compute ourselves

Every price comes from our own swept market data, with its source shown. Ships the market never lists are priced from confirmed contract sales, abyssals from their own rolls, blueprints from the full material tree.

Contract search

Every public contract in New Eden in one search, all regions at once, refreshed every 30 minutes. Each contract is appraised line by line, and deals are flagged on the row.

Appraisal

Paste a list, a fit, or a cargo scan and every line comes back priced with its source shown. No account needed.

Intel on the map

Axon reads a member's in-game intel chat and posts parsed reports to shared channels, and channel reports draw on the universe map. Gate camps are counted from ship kills only.

Corporation tools

Directors author doctrines in the fitting tool itself. Ship replacement values the loss with the same pricing models, and the kill behind the claim is already in the system.
