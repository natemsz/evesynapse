Trust

Your keys, your call.

Prices come from EveSynapse's own swept market data, and every price shows its source. You stay in charge of your characters and your data.

You pick the scopes

Linking a character starts with the features you want: the sign-in asks for the scopes behind those features, and no more. Re-linking the same character never takes a permission away.

Encrypted at rest

Refresh tokens are sealed with AES-256-GCM, the current standard for encryption at rest. The encryption keys are stored off-server, on secure hardware. Reading your data never means trusting us with the keys to it.

Public data, no login

Tools using public data require no login at all to use: the fitting simulator, planners, market and intel tools, and the universe map, are open to everyone. Sign in only when you want your own characters in the picture.

Or run it yourself

Open source, one setup script, your own ESI application and your own data. The public site runs the same build you can download.

PKCE on every sign-in

The EVE login flow is PKCE-protected: the sign-in code can only be exchanged by the browser that asked for it, so an intercepted code is worthless to anyone else.

Revoke on unlink

When you unlink a character, you can fully revoke its token with EVE itself, right in the unlink flow. Deleting our copy is the floor; killing the token at the source is your call.
