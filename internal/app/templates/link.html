Take intel with you: Axon

Axon is the desktop companion for EveSynapse intel, and this sign-up turns on the character location it files reports by. It watches your EVE chat logs and sends new lines to your intel channels while you play, so sightings reach the map on their own. Its overlay puts reporting on a shortcut: a small translucent paste field where an empty send files what is on your clipboard, showing the report type and system it detected before anything is sent, either of them yours to override.
