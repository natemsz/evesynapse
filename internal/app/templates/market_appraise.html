Appraise a paste

Paste a list of items — a fit, a cargo scan, an asset window, a loot haul — and every line is priced from The Forge's swept prices and EveSynapse's own stored estimates. Each price says where it came from and when it was current. Lines that cannot be priced say so; nothing is filled in with a guess. Up to 1,000 lines at a time.

Plain lists, one item per line, with or without quantities; EFT fits; and copies from the game's asset and cargo windows all work. Blueprint lines can carry their facts in brackets, like Rifter Blueprint (BPC, ME 10, TE 20, 5 runs). Mutated (abyssal) items price from a paste when the line carries the item's link from the game — the link names the item itself, so the line prices that item from its stored rolls estimate when EveSynapse holds one for it. A mutated line without its link, or an item with no estimate on record, prices its base item and says so; mutated items are browsable from their rolls in Contract Search.
