Paste a list of items — a fit, a cargo scan, an asset window, a loot haul — and every line is priced from The Forge's swept prices and EveSynapse's own stored estimates. Each price says where it came from and when it was current. Lines that cannot be priced say so; nothing is filled in with a guess. Up to 1,000 lines at a time.

Load a sample paste:
Cargo / Assets [EFT Fittings] BPC [ME/TE/Runs] Abyssal Links Moon Survey
Format rules

Plain lists, one item per line, with or without quantities; copies from the game's asset and cargo windows; and EFT fits all work. A pasted EFT fit appraises the ship, its fitted modules and charges, and the drones and cargo the fit lists. A moon survey paste — ore names with their percentages — appraises each ore at its survey share of 10,000 units. Blueprint lines can carry their facts in brackets, like Rifter Blueprint (BPC, ME 10, TE 20, 5 runs). A copy prices from the median asking price of copy listings in our contract corpus — never from the original's market price — and a copy with too few listings on record is left unpriced. Mutated (abyssal) items appraise too: when the line carries the item's link from the game, the line prices that item from its stored rolls estimate. A mutated line without its link, or an item with no estimate on record, prices its base item and says so.
