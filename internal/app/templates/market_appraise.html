Paste a list of items — a fit, a cargo scan, an asset window, a loot haul — and every line is priced from The Forge's swept prices and EveSynapse's own stored estimates. Each price says where it came from and when it was current. Lines that cannot be priced say so; nothing is filled in with a guess. Up to 1,000 lines at a time.

Plain lists, one item per line, with or without quantities; EFT fits; and copies from the game's asset and cargo windows all work. Blueprint lines can carry their facts in brackets, like Rifter Blueprint (BPC, ME 10, TE 20, 5 runs). Mutated (abyssal) items cannot be priced from a paste — a paste carries no rolls — so a mutated line prices its base item and says so; the mutated item itself is priced from its rolls in Contract Search.
