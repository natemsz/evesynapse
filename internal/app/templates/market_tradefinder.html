A route buys from sell orders in {{$s.OriginName}} and lists the items for sale in {{$s.DestName}}. Profit per item is the typical sell price in {{$s.DestName}} minus the typical sell price in {{$s.OriginName}}. Sold per day is the average daily amount that actually traded in {{$s.DestName}} over the last seven recorded days. Estimated profit per day is the profit per item times whichever runs out first: what {{$s.DestName}} sells each day, or the stock on sale in {{$s.OriginName}}. Before broker fees, sales tax and hauling.
