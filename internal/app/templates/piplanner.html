Production map

Pick any P1 to P4 tile to plan it. Gold is the target, green its chain inputs, blue what it feeds; the rest is faded. With planets ticked, chain tiles your planets cannot supply are flagged. Yields: Basic turns 3,000 R0 into 20 P1 every 30 minutes; Advanced turns 40 + 40 P1 into 5 P2, or 10 of each P2 into 3 P3, every hour; High-Tech turns 6 of each input into 1 P4 every hour.
