Corroborated: one offer fits the LP (and the ISK, when the wallet shows it) and the items turned up in your assets. Inferred: one offer fits but the items weren't seen, perhaps already sold or moved. Ambiguous: several offers fit; pick one below. Unexplained: no single offer fits, so only the LP spent is counted. Net ISK and ISK per LP cover items sold, from corroborated, inferred and picked purchases, with LP costing nothing: LP is earned, not bought.
