LP storesiWhat your loyalty points bought, and what those items earned when they sold. ESI shows your LP balances but no store purchases, so each purchase is worked out from a drop in a balance between two hourly readings and cannot be timed closer than that. Corroborated: one offer fits the LP (and the ISK, when the wallet shows it) and the items turned up in your assets. Inferred: one offer fits but the items were not seen, perhaps already sold or moved. Ambiguous: several offers fit; pick one below. Unexplained: no single offer fits, so only the LP spent counts. Net ISK and ISK per LP cover sold items from corroborated, inferred and picked purchases, with LP costing nothing: LP is earned, not bought. The other lines show the same net if each LP had cost that much, which is what the LP could have made elsewhere. Corporation LP is not available from ESI.
