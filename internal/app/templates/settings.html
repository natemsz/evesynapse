    Using it

    Send the key in a header: Authorization: Bearer esk_…. Each key gets {{.PerMinute}} requests a minute with a short burst, a daily allowance, and up to {{.MaxBatch}} items per request.

    • GET {{.BaseURL}}/prices?region=10000002&types=34,35
      Prices with where each came from, the full order book statistics, and trends.
    • GET {{.BaseURL}}/history?region=10000002&type=34&days=90
      A type's daily figures from our sweeps.
    • GET {{.BaseURL}}/changes?region=10000002&days=1
      Sharp price moves, supply drops and spreads widening.
    • POST {{.BaseURL}}/appraise
      Price a list: {"lines":[{"type_id":23919,"quantity":1,"me":10,"te":20},{"type_id":47781,"quantity":1,"item_id":1040000000000}]}. Abyssal modules by item id, blueprint copies with "copy":true and runs, researched blueprints by ME and TE.
    • GET {{.BaseURL}}/abyssal/{type_id}/{item_id}
      One abyssal module's estimate. A module we haven't seen yet is queued and priced within a few minutes.
    • GET {{.BaseURL}}/key
      Your key's allowance and today's use.
    {{end}}
